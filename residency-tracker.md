### Notes on the two that nearly work

**Gasworks** is the one to chase. The funding package is the strongest available to an India-based
early-career artist: stipend, materials budget, studio, shared accommodation, return flights, visa
support, travel card. The 2026-03-02 date has passed, so either it belongs to the cycle just gone or
it was recorded from a stale page. Confirm the live call date before anything else.

**Palazzo Monti** is the only thing you could apply to this month. It gives a private studio and
bedroom in Brescia and matches the material vocabulary well, but it pays nothing and charges €25 to
apply. Travel, food and return shipping fall on the artist. Worth doing only if the goal shifts from
income to line-on-the-CV, and only if someone is funding the travel.
