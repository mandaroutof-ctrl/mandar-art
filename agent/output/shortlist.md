## Watch out for

**The Inlaks Residency at Skowhegan (2027)**

- Outside this run's region (USA). Recorded because the call is India-specific and open now.
- Age cap: 35 or younger in the year of application, on both the Inlaks and Skowhegan wording. Client's age unconfirmed.
- The fee waiver is not automatic. Skowhegan: 'Before submitting your application, please send us an email at help@skowheganart.org.' Proof of nationality, residency and age is required, so allow time before 16 October.
- No visa help. Skowhegan: 'We are unable to assist with the application for a Visa or cover the associated expenses, but we can provide a letter of invitation upon request... The majority of our international participants come to Skowhegan on a tourist visa.' US interview waits in India can be long.
- Tuition is USD 6,000 per person. Skowhegan is need-blind and says 'no accepted artists will be turned away for lack of funding', but the tuition conversation is separate from the Inlaks grant and should be settled in writing.
- Rejected if the artist has held any prior Inlaks overseas grant.

**Braunschweig Projects Fellowship 2027/28 (HBK Braunschweig)**

- Graduation window is strict: art degree completed between 1 April 2016 and 1 April 2023.
- Fellow pays EUR 250 per month back towards living costs.
- Cannot hold other funding during the fellowship.
- Studio provision beyond the studio apartment not confirmed on the page.

**Artist in Residence at The Factory (Factory on Willow) 2027**

- Official: 'The program requires for each artist to donate once piece created during their residency to be part of the Factory on Willow's permanent collection.' The donated work is displayed across the campus.
- Obligation: 'Each residency concludes with an artist opening celebration' acting as a solo or duo exhibition; artists are also expected to pursue local connections themselves.
- USD 350 travel will not cover a flight from India; the USD 1,600 has to stretch across food and materials for up to 12 weeks.
- The official page says the deadline is rolling and spots fill quickly, so the ACA date is a backstop, not a safe target.
- Visa: nothing on the official site about visas, invitation letters or work authorisation.

**SIXI AiR (Sixi Museum) International Artist-in-Residence Project 2027, Spring and Autumn**

- Museum's own slide: 'The artist is expected to donate a selection of the artworks created during the residency to Sixi Museum.'
- Only 4 places across two seasons, 2 artists in residence at a time.
- No living allowance is listed among the covered costs, so day-to-day food and spending money look like the artist's own. Confirm before applying.
- Obligations: 'residents should participate in events including artist talk, workshop and open studio' and the project 'expects for tangible and presentable outcome'.
- Proposals must address one of the two named themes.
- Chinese visa for an Indian passport holder: the museum covers the visa application fee and warns 'International artists should allow sufficient time for visa processing at the time of application, usually at least 2-3 months in advance.'

- The foundation looks for 'a fully developed, mature artistic voice' and a track record of exhibitions, awards or grants, which is a high bar for an emerging artist.
- This is the final open public call; from July 2027 selection moves off an open call.
- Fellows may not take gainful employment that would prevent focus on the project during the residency.

**NIROX Foundation Residency**

- The funded route for Indians is the Inlaks Residency at NIROX, but its 2026 cycle is closed (residency 1 October to 12 November 2026; shortlist notified late July 2026). Watch Inlaks for the 2027 call.
- South African visa required for Indian passport holders; nothing on the page about visa support.

**Casa de Velázquez / Académie de France à Madrid Artists in Residence 2027-2028**

## How the screen works

**Hard gates.** A residency is thrown out if it pays no stipend or grant, is closed to India-based artists, is digital-only, excludes both mixed media and sculpture, requires a duo or collective, is for established artists only, or needs more years of practice than the client has. Unknown is not the same as failing, so an unknown goes to the verify list instead.

**Fit score.** Residencies that pass the gates are scored out of 100.

| Dimension | Points | What earns them |
|---|---|---|
| Funding strength | 30 | A living stipend beats a production-only grant. Travel, housing, studio and materials money add. Fees and artist-paid costs subtract. |
| India access | 20 | India-specific calls score highest, then open international calls. Visa support adds. |
| Country practicality | 15 | Visa friction, travel from India and cost of living for an Indian passport holder. |
| Discipline fit | 15 | Welcomes sculpture, installation and mixed media. Workshops add. A required single material or an off-theme constraint subtracts. |
| Style resonance | 10 | Programme leans towards surreal, figurative, material or conceptual work rather than digital or social practice. |
| Emerging fit | 10 | Explicitly for emerging or early-career artists. A multi-year experience minimum subtracts. |

Tiers: Strong fit 75+ · Good fit 60+ · Possible 45+.
